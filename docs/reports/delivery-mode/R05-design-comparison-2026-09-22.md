## Alternatives measured

| Alternative | Enclosed area | Covered area | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Current single-storey double-loaded bar with protected patio and 1.50 m gallery | 784.881 m² | 918.563 m² | Selected |
| Fragmented/clustered two-wing courtyard inspired by the pavilions references | about 1050 m² | not re-established in the current run | Rejected for the current programme |

The adopted bar is inside the official 783–814 m² enclosed estimate and the
850–950 m² covered estimate, while retaining 626 m² net internal and 260 m²
external programme. The two-wing alternative is about 236 m² above the upper
enclosed estimate (roughly 30 percent), before the added cost and coordination of
a second gallery and envelope. A two-storey compact option was also rejected:
the 626 m² useful programme and the adopted enclosed range do not justify the
extra vertical circulation in this study.

## Visual-reference reconciliation

The three package images were used only as `DESIGN_REFERENCE`. The current
solution adopts their transferable principles: a legible public arrival and
administrative/triage face, a protected residential/patio face, a covered
gallery as the readable path, a privacy gradient, and garden/landscape as part
of the experience rather than leftover space. It does not copy the images'
dimensions, terrain polygon, street names, north, scale, number of pavilions,
curves, or facade details.

The fragmented alternative remains a valid future revision path if verified
site data or a new programme changes the measurable envelope. It is not frozen
into R05 because its area penalty is material and its supposed block separation
cannot be checked without the real boundary, setbacks and north.
